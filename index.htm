About

Research that connects theory and practice

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at William Paterson University in New Jersey and the director of the ACBOS Virtual Research Laboratory. My work bridges theoretical foundations in reinforcement learning and learning automata with practical applications in decentralized systems and autonomous decision-making.

Before joining William Paterson in 2023, I held postdoctoral positions at Stevens Institute of Technology and earned my Ph.D. and M.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Amirkabir University of Technology. I also serve as a reviewer and guest editor for journals and conferences in my field.
